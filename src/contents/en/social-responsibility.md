As part of its social responsibility efforts, AILab LLC took part as a sponsor of the "First National Artificial Intelligence Olympiad", held in Mongolia for the first time in 2025 on the initiative of the Ministry of Digital Development, Innovation and Communications and the Department of Information and Computer Sciences at the National University of Mongolia (NUM).

Selected through the Olympiad, a total of 8 students in two teams represented their country at the international competition, where 284 students from 61 countries spent two days competing on 6 problems in machine learning and artificial intelligence. As a result, the Mongolian teams achieved the proud distinction of winning two bronze medals.

This achievement was a significant milestone, showing that Mongolia now has the foundation to nurture education and talent in artificial intelligence and to compete at the international level.

We also took part as General Sponsor of the "Mongolian Information Technology–2025" scientific conference. At a time when artificial intelligence is developing rapidly, we are truly delighted to be making a tangible contribution to preparing children and young people, giving them the right career guidance, and supporting students' knowledge, skills and growth.

Going forward, AILab LLC will continue, as part of its social responsibility, to consistently support information technology and artificial intelligence education and to help prepare the skilled workforce of the future.
