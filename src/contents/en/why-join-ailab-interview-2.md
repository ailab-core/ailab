**Hello! Could you tell us a bit about yourself?**

Hello. My name is Shinenbayar Gansukh, and I work as a Senior Developer. I am responsible for the software side of the project I oversee, and I prepare my team members' work plans and the technical solutions.

**Why did you join AILab?**

The opportunities for growth and the strong support from management, but what influenced me most was the chance to work alongside peers my own age.

**The advantages of working at AILab**

The company always offers every kind of support for taking initiative and provides more flexible working arrangements.

**What are you focusing on these days?**

To improve development quality, I am focused on introducing a variety of testing methodologies into my project.

**What does a typical workday look like for you?**

I start my morning with a hot coffee and begin work with our Daily Stand Up. In the morning hours I focus on new technologies and research, and in the afternoon I dedicate myself fully to development.

**What do you enjoy doing in your free time, outside of work?**

I enjoy playing various sports and exploring new experiences.

**What are your dreams and goals?**

I aim to become a skilled professional who has made a clear contribution of my own to my country.
