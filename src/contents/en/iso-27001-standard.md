**AILab LLC** has successfully implemented the international Information Security Management System (ISMS)

standard ISO/IEC 27001 in its operations since 2024, confirming that it fully ensures information security and operates in compliance with international standards,

and it also successfully passed its 2025 certification audit.

ISO/IEC 27001 is the international standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS), implemented and officially recognized in more than 140 countries worldwide. The standard aims to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of an organization's information, and is built on management principles of systematically assessing risks and managing them appropriately.
